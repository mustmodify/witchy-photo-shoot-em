# Witchy Photo Shoot with Em

A single-page review album of the 413 frames picked from the 5 October 2026 witchy photo shoot. Open a photo, note its four-digit frame number, and send the numbers you love and the ones to drop.

The frames were picked and developed in Storylight, rendered from the raw files at 1600 px on the long edge, then re-encoded as web JPEGs with 560 px thumbnails. The page is plain HTML generated by `scripts/build_html.py` from `chapters.json` and `manifest.json`. The raw files are not in this repository.
